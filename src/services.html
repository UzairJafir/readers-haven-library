Our Services

Everything Readers' Haven offers to help you read, learn, study and connect, in the library and online.

In-Library Services

Book Lending

Borrow up to 10 books at a time for 3 weeks, with easy renewals at the desk or online.

Study Rooms

Quiet individual desks and bookable group rooms with whiteboards and free Wi-Fi.

Computer & Printing

Public computers, scanning, photocopying and printing at low cost for members and visitors.

Reference Help

Our librarians help you find sources, research a topic or choose your next read.

Digital Services

E-Books & Audiobooks

Download thousands of titles to your phone, tablet or e-reader with your library card.

Online Catalogue

Search the collection, reserve items and manage your account from home, any time.

Research Databases

Free access to journals, newspapers and learning resources for students and adults.

Programs & Community

Children's Story Hour

Weekend storytelling, crafts and reading games for young readers.

Book Clubs

Monthly clubs for teens and adults, with a new title and lively discussion every time.

Workshops

Digital literacy, resume writing and creative writing sessions led by local experts.

Accessibility Support

Large-print books, assistive technology and home delivery for members who cannot visit.
